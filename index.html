RazaFlix privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

What RazaFlix is

RazaFlix is a media player for TV. It does not provide, sell or host any channels, movies, series or playlists. You sign in with the Xtream Codes details of a service that you already have a right to use, and RazaFlix plays what that service sends.

What we collect

We do not collect, store or sell any personal data on our own servers. RazaFlix has no account system and no analytics or advertising.

What is stored on your TV

The following stays only on your TV and is never sent to us: the profile name, server address, username and password you enter; your My List, Continue Watching positions and settings; and a temporary copy of the channel and title lists so the app starts faster. Removing a profile or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Services the app contacts

  • Your IPTV provider: your login details and requests for lists and streams go to the server you entered. Its own privacy policy applies.
  • Artwork and details (optional): title names may be sent to The Movie Database (TMDB) to show cast and information, if you add a TMDB key in Settings.
  • Subtitles (optional): if you turn on subtitle search and add your own key, the title name and your account details for that subtitle service are sent to that service only (for example SubDL or OpenSubtitles).

Children

RazaFlix does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children. Content seen in the app comes from your provider. Use the adult-category filter in Settings to hide adult categories.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new date appears at the top of this page.
